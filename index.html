Research Proposal
Intro to Research Methods
Sep-14-2016

Title: Why do people use photography and graphic design as a weapon against war?

Topic: Digital media. It refers to audio, video, and photo content that has been encoded or digitally compressed (Microsoft). Most people use media in various ways, and it focuses on the audience themselves and how they decide to use digital media. An example of this is the question of why people use photography and graphic design as a weapon against war.

Background: Photography is a collection of journalism, nature, portrait, and art photos that help in fighting violence. It gives the photographer the ability to speak out through a one-click device, to visualize the truth, and to help justice win against injustice. Since the invention of photography, it has existed as a medium that helps the human eye tell the truth and the lie apart. An example of this function is the heartbreaking picture of a dead refugee baby on a Turkish beach in September this year. Photography is a form of journalism, known as photojournalism. Photojournalism is the way of creating news articles using mainly photographs. It plays a vital role in society in the fight against violence. One click is all it takes to make a video or a picture that helps people understand what is being done to someone in the news. But photographs look the same unless they get a fine touch in Adobe Photoshop and other design tools. This is why I will go more deeply into how photography and design have changed the world, are changing it, and will keep changing it. I will not only look into photography and design; I will also look at the war and epidemic crime happening now. This is why photography and graphic design are emerging around the world against war.

Research Problem: The research aims to determine why people use photography and graphic design as a weapon against war.

Sub-problems:

  1. What makes photography and graphic design different from guns and bombs?
  2. Does the information shared in photography and graphic design against war differ from the photographs we usually click?
  3. Do people capture war photographs and design them for the same reasons we usually capture photographs, or for different reasons?

Research Question: The purpose of this study is to determine why people use photography and graphic design as a weapon against war by investigating why people capture and design war photographs, and why we usually capture photographs such as selfies.

Aim: The aim of this pilot study is to determine the reasons why people capture and design war photographs, and why we usually capture photographs, by specifically looking at photographers who take war photographs and regular photographs.

Methodology: This study will be a subjective research study. Subjective research techniques endeavor to find what is thought to be a dynamic reality, concentrating primarily on understanding the specifics of photography and design that are used against war. I will use some photographs and designs as examples of digital media against war. Significance: The exponential increase in the use of photographs and design around the world has led to the emergence of research on digital media against war. These studies vary between photographers who take war photos and the role that design plays in photography. These researchers have tried to determine why people take war photos as opposed to war, why people go to war, and why people should use digital media.

Why Hope

The following are the reasons why we hope photography and graphic design will change the world:

1

To Make a Difference

We know that everyone hopes to make a difference, but fighting wars and creating chaos does not make the difference we think it will. When faced with making a difference, we are not aware of what the outcome will be. That’s why we need to hope that photography and graphic design will make a difference around the world, not the difference we imagine by fighting, but one that comes from showing the reality of what is going on.

2

To Give Strength

As humans, we are taught to have self-confidence, but most of us are confident that we can make a better day by using violence against that one child. When we hope in photography and graphic design, we can find the confidence and strength to face any challenge that comes our way. We can find the confidence and strength to believe that violence will end through photography and graphic design.

3

To Bring Happiness

When we try to make a difference by using violence, it does not bring happiness to us. It also does not bring happiness when we win a battle by killing a terrorist. But when someone takes a picture of a child who is affected by war, it gives us a clue about how to help. Then, when we edit those photographs, they help us make sure that the child is out of the danger zone and can live like us. That brings happiness to him or her, and it also brings happiness to us, because that child is safe now.

4

To Teach Us Patience

We all encourage one another to solve our own problems. Instead of solving them, we are creating more problems by bombing that one child. In that situation, we realize that we face an impossible or hopeless situation, and we must learn how photography and graphic design teach us to be patient and to have the strength to believe that one day things will be better for that one child. We do not need to be anxious or worried, as we are when we use violence. We can find peace in the ability of photography and graphic design to show us what is real.

5

To Create a Better Day

When we bring happiness to that one child, we start bringing happiness to others like him or her. To do that, we do not need to use violence; violence creates bias between people. Photography will show what is happening, and graphic design will predict how we can create a better day, not by bombing but by helping that one child and giving him or her the strength to live and to fight against violence without using violence.

6

To Bring Encouragement

We all know the feeling we get at the end when we use violence against an enemy, but that violence wounds many children like that one child. Now think about how photography and graphic design can help those children, like that one child. We can find encouragement and joy amid life’s struggles because we know we will stay alive when we aren’t using violence.

Because of photography and graphic design, we have hope to make a difference, bring happiness, create a better day, give ourselves strength and be patient, for eternity.

Photo-Graphic

Photography

Photography is an art that addresses everyone who is willing to notice the details and experience life from another point of view. It’s not easy seeing through a lens, distorting, magnifying or editing reality. When you manage to achieve it though, you know how your life changes!

Courtesy of: http://www.printl.net/

War

War is the use of violence and force between two or more states to resolve a matter of dispute.

Courtesy of: http://www.duhaime.org/

But photo-graphic is the use of photographs and words to resolve the dispute, show what is real, and then help make things better.

Graphic Design

Graphic design, also known as communication design, is the art and practice of planning and projecting ideas and experiences with visual and textual content. The form of the communication can be physical or virtual, and may include images, words, or graphic forms. The experience can take place in an instant or over a long period of time. The work can happen at any scale, from the design of a single postage stamp to a national postal signage system, or from a company’s digital avatar to the sprawling and interlinked digital and physical content of an international newspaper. It can also be for any purpose, whether commercial, educational, cultural, or political.

Courtesy of: http://www.aiga.org/

Social Work

Social work is a helping profession; the main goal of social work is to improve a society’s overall well-being, especially for the most vulnerable populations.

Courtesy of: http://www.cosw.sc.edu/

So, social workers see photography and graphic design as a method of helping someone who needs help. They believe that those photographs and graphic designs help them and will help others, in order to help those in need.

Peace and Safe

Peace is when people are able to resolve their conflicts without violence and can work together to improve the quality of their lives.

Courtesy of: http://www.international-alert.org/

Safe means secure from liability to harm, injury, danger, or risk.

Courtesy of: http://www.dictionary.com/

We can make a safe and peaceful place if we start changing our minds and stop bombing innocent people. This is why we need photo-graphic.

About Project

If you ever ask college students whether they would want to take pictures, very few would say yes. I would, not simply because I like taking pictures or enjoy spending hours and hours outside in nature, but because I could use one click to change a world full of violence, crime, and conflict. Along with this, I feel that we are all human beings trying to change the world, and most people are preparing to make this beautiful world peaceful. They use war to diminish and end violence, crime, and conflict in order to make a peaceful world, but they always fail. That is why I chose photography as the model or example to change the world.

College students today are highly prepared for war in order to make the world a peaceful place to live, but they don’t know that one click could possibly change the world into a more peaceful one. Students get thrown into a war setting by themselves, which is a major shock to them because of the major wars in different places around the world. Maybe joining a war and fighting the enemy on a peaceful mission wouldn’t be as much of a change.

Many high school students take the opportunity to join the Army, the US Navy, and the US Marines to help create a new world with peace. Instead of creating a new world with peace, they are helping enemies like the Taliban, ISIS, and Boko Haram modify the old world into a new world with more violence and more crime. We students need to join the photography community. So we should join the photography community to change the world in one click. And this is what this project is about.
